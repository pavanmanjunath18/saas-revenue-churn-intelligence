# Data Generation Design — SaaS Revenue & Churn Intelligence Platform

## Overview

Synthetic data generated by `scripts/generate_mock_data.py`.  
1,500 customers · 24 months (Jan 2024 – Dec 2025) · 50,068 total rows across 12 tables.

---

## Important: Intentional Rate Amplification

Churn and at-risk rates are **deliberately amplified above real-world SaaS benchmarks**
so that meaningful patterns are visible at portfolio dataset scale (1,500 customers).

| Metric | Real-world range | This dataset |
|---|---|---|
| SMB monthly churn (healthy) | 2–4% | 4.5% |
| SMB monthly churn (at-risk) | 4–8% | 11% |
| Mid-market monthly churn | 1–3% | 2–6% |
| Enterprise monthly churn | 0.2–1% | 0.5–1.8% |
| Payment failure rate | 1–3% | 3–4% |

A real dataset of this size with real-world churn rates would show weak trends in a 24-month window.
The amplification is intentional and documented.  All behavioral correlations remain directionally correct.

## Behavioral Profiles

Assigned at customer creation. Drives all downstream signal generation.

| Profile | % (SMB) | % (Mid-market) | % (Enterprise) |
|---|---|---|---|
| healthy | 50% | 65% | 80% |
| at_risk | 30% | 25% | 15% |
| pre_churned | 20% | 10% | 5% |

`pre_churned` customers have a randomly assigned churn target month (3–18 months after signup).
Usage begins declining 2–4 months before churn. Support ticket severity spikes in the final 1–2 months.

---

## Health Score (per customer per month)

A composite score (0.0–1.0) computed each month from actual generated signals.
This score directly drives churn, upgrade, and payment failure probabilities.

| Signal | Weight | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Usage trend (3-month rolling) | 30% | product_usage |
| Payment success last month | 25% | payments |
| Support ticket severity last month | 20% | support_tickets |
| Customer tenure | 15% | subscriptions.started_at |
| Feature adoption breadth | 10% | product_usage.features_used_count |
| Annual billing bonus | +0.08 | subscriptions.billing_interval |

**Noise:** ±12% random multiplier applied to health score each month so signals are real but not artificially perfect.

---

## Correlated Churn Logic

Churn probability is NOT purely random. It is derived from:

```
churn_p = base_churn_rate
          × interval_multiplier     (0.4 if annual, 1.0 if monthly)
          × health_churn_mult        (0.3 if health=1.0, up to 2.5 if health=0.0)
          × noise                    (±15%)
```

Result: a healthy annual enterprise customer has ~0.07% monthly churn probability.
An at-risk monthly SMB customer with failed payments and declining usage can reach ~25% monthly churn.

---

## Correlated Expansion Logic

Upgrades are NOT random. They require:

```
upgrade_p = base_upgrade_rate
            × health_upgrade_mult   (0.3 if health=0.0, 2.0 if health=1.0)
            × tenure_bonus          (up to +60% for 14+ months)
            × noise                 (±15%)
```

A brand-new unhealthy customer has near-zero upgrade probability.
A healthy 12-month customer on a mid-market plan has a meaningful upgrade probability each month.

## Revenue Events

| Event | How generated |
|---|---|
| New subscription | On customer signup |
| Upgrade | Correlated with high health score + tenure. Old sub canceled, new sub created. |
| Downgrade | Correlated with low health score. Same mechanism as upgrade. |
| Churn | Correlated with health score + billing interval + behavioral profile |
| Reactivation | ~3.5% chance per month after 3+ months inactive |
| Discount | 20–50% of customers (varies by segment) — applied at signup |
| Failed payment | Correlated with health score; retried within 3–7 days |
| Refund | ~1.8% of successful payments |

## Usage Signal Decay (Pre-churn Pattern)

For `pre_churned` customers:

- Months until churn > 4: mild decline, factor ~0.55–1.0
- 4 months before churn: factor drops to 0.65
- 3 months: 0.49
- 2 months: 0.33
- 1 month: 0.17
- Churn month: 0.04 (near-zero activity)

This produces the realistic "usage cliff" pattern that churn risk models can detect.

## Dataset Size Rationale

| Table | Rows | Why |
|---|---|---|
| customers | 1,500 | Enough for statistically meaningful cohorts (~62/month avg) |
| subscriptions | 2,435 | 935 extra rows from upgrades/downgrades |
| invoices | 10,595 | ~7 months avg active tenure per customer |
| product_usage | 12,237 | Monthly snapshots for all active periods |
| support_tickets | 2,785 | ~1.9 tickets/customer on average |

Total: **50,068 rows**. Large enough for dashboard trends, small enough for fast SQL queries.
